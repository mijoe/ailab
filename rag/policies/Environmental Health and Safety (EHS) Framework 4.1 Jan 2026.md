# Policy: Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) Framework
**Effective Date:** Jan 2026 | **Scope:** Global Facilities | **Version:** 4.1

## 1. Purpose
This policy ensures a safe working environment for employees and visitors. In the context of RAG testing, this document serves as the primary reference for **Physical Safety** vs. **Logical Security**.

## 5. Contractor and Vendor Site Access
### 5.1 Pre-Arrival Requirements
Every third-party vendor requiring physical access to a corporate facility (e.g., Data Center maintenance, Electrical work) must submit a **Method Statement** and **Risk Assessment (RAMS)** to the Facilities Manager 72 hours prior to arrival.

### 5.2 On-site Safety Protocols (The "Yellow Badge" System)
* **Escorted Access:** Vendors are issued a "Yellow Badge," which signifies they must be escorted by an "All-Access" (Blue Badge) employee at all times.
* **Restricted Areas:** Access to "Hazardous Zones" (Mechanical rooms, Battery arrays) requires specific PPE, including steel-toed boots and high-visibility vests.
* **Hot Work Permits:** Any vendor performing activities that generate heat, sparks, or open flames (welding, grinding) must obtain a signed "Hot Work Permit." A "Fire Watch" individual must remain on-site for 60 minutes after the work is completed.

### 5.3 Incident Reporting (Physical)
Any physical injury sustained by a vendor on-site must be reported via the *EHS Portal* within 1 hour. This is distinct from a "Security Incident" (which is reported to the SOC).
